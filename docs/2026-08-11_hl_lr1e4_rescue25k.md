## Preregistered scope

Only candidate B from the 064_00 2K mechanism sweep was eligible: change only `learning_rate` to `1e-4`. The effective fixed values were `net_arch=[64,64]`, `gamma=1`, `gae_lambda=1`, `n_steps=144`, `batch_size=144`, `n_epochs=5`, `ent_coef=0.01`, and `clip_range=0.2`. The maximum was 25,000 training steps, with checkpoints fixed in advance at 5K, 10K and 25K. The gate required: no off-grid or request-to-safe/raw-to-safe/safe-to-DSSAT mismatch, at least three nonzero action pairs, action after DAP1, and more than one cross-year action signature. `WP_ET` could be reported only from a same-definition ETCP/snapshot; otherwise it would be marked unavailable and not inferred. This is a rescue diagnostic, not a post-hoc paper checkpoint-selection rule and never authorizes a 100K run.

## Decision gate

The lr=1e-4 branch passes the 25K mechanism gate and has mean validation yield 6824.86 kg/ha. Against the earlier frozen HL 25K replay (about 6844.3 kg/ha), it is not evidence of performance improvement. It shows only that this single-seed rescue branch retained action transmission and non-singleton cross-year behavior at 25K. **Do not approve 100K.** Before any extension, preregister an independent-seed/time-out validation selection rule and add a same-definition ETCP source; do not treat this post-hoc rescue as a final paper checkpoint or generalization result.
