## First-read interpretation

- All three seeds completed, so this is a valid multiseed check for LCA2010 under the 032 stress-aware reward.
- The 032_03 seed0 positive signal is not seed-stable. Seed0 selects the 50k checkpoint (`I45/N160`, yield `8337.90`, max NSTRES `0.012`), but seed1 selects 25k with `I45/N120` and lower yield (`7924.38`, max NSTRES `0.295`), while seed2 selects 200k with `I45/N80` and much lower yield (`7166.19`, max NSTRES `0.403`).
- Longer training to 200k does not solve stability. Seed0 and seed1 final 200k are worse than their selected mid-training checkpoints; seed2 final 200k is selected by reward but has high nitrogen stress and low yield.
- The current reward still over-rewards nitrogen saving/PFP_N relative to yield preservation and nitrogen-stress control in some seeds. It can select under-fertilized policies with high PFP_N but many NSTRES days.
- Do not expand this exact 032 reward/configuration to all years/stations as a final framework.
- Next candidate direction should explicitly address reward-selection mismatch: selected checkpoint should include a yield/stress guardrail, or the reward should penalize accumulated nitrogen stress/yield loss more directly. This requires a new pre-registered task, not an in-place tweak.
