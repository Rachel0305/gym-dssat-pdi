 | Management performance and interannual variability by site and training regime.** Values report H and A group means, absolute differences (A−H), percentage differences relative to H, and archived exploratory 95% bootstrap intervals for absolute differences. Welch intervals are supplied only for interannual SD, as audited. Yield, irrigation, N application, PFP_N and WP_ET are averaged over valid years within each policy, then across the eight policies. PFP_N is the mean of annual yield/N ratios, not the ratio of mean yield to mean N; zero-N annual values remain undefined rather than zero. WP_ET is the mean of annual yield/(10×ETCP) ratios (kg m−3). Interannual SD is computed separately for each policy over ten years (ddof=1) and then averaged across policies. Missing interval fields mean not supplied for that statistic, not zero uncertainty. Intervals are unadjusted for multiple comparisons.


For PFP_N, the FQ historical group and YC augmented group each have seven defined policies: FQ H seed5 and YC A seed6 applied no N and their PFP_N values are undefined. All other groups have eight defined PFP_N policies. Group means and relative changes retain this missingness convention and should not be read as a complete-eight-policy N-efficiency improvement.
