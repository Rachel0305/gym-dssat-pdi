**Figure D1 | Historical, augmented and evaluation weather over a fixed crop-management window.** Columns represent sites and rows show total precipitation and the longest dry spell over DAP 0–90 (91 days). H denotes historical-training input years, A distinct archived augmented windows, and T evaluation-input years (2014–2023). DAP is aligned to the frozen representative planting date; YC recovered series use the final DAP=0 anchor because their legacy dates are not calendar dates. Dry days have RAIN≤0 mm. Boxes show the median and interquartile range, whiskers extend to 1.5×IQR, and all distinct-window points are displayed. n indicates distinct numeric weather windows, not independent climate replicates. The y-axis scales differ between sites. SY/LC A represents verified selected-bank windows, not confirmed full-window daily encounters; HL/FQ A uses archived daily episodes complete over this window; YC A is the 78-realization verified subset, excluding two unresolved candidates. Duplicate windows are displayed once. Coverage results are descriptive, sample-size-dependent and conditional on source/QC restrictions; they do not establish PPO generalization or mechanisms. FQ2018 uses the archived quality-control version. Late-season weather beyond DAP90 is outside scope.**
